Blind Drop Sealed

One song each. No names attached.

A daily guessing game for one private group of friends. Everyone drops a song before 8:00 PM — blind, with no idea what anyone else picked. At 8:00 the songs come out as a numbered list and you have two hours to work out who dropped what.

A round, end to end

Four steps, every day

  1. Until 8

    Drop a song

    Search, pick one, seal it. You cannot see anyone else's, and nobody can see how many people have dropped — not even a count.

  2. 8:00 PM

    The reveal

    Every song appears at once as an anonymous numbered list. Only people who dropped a song get to guess.

  3. 8–10 PM

    Name them

    Assign a person to each number and lock in. Change your mind as often as you like until the clock runs out.

  4. 10:00 PM

    Answers

    Who dropped what, who guessed right, and how unreadable you were. Every song joins the group's record, exportable to Apple Music or Spotify.
